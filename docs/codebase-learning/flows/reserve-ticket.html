Reserve a ticket

An admitted shopper adds one ticket in Seat Selection. Ticketing checks that the shopper still has access and that the requested quantity fits their account limit. It takes one unit out of available stock and records a hold for that shopping session. The shopper receives the quantity now held and when it expires. They can use the hold at checkout, where order creation is a separate operation.
