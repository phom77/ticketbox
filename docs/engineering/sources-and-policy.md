# Sources and policy scope

These guides adapt the AI Engineering Starter Kit v3.2 to TicketBox. Apply
them to the affected change. Use the repository's accepted architecture,
contracts, requirements and ADRs as the authority for project-specific behavior.
Existing source and verification show the current state. Report discrepancies
between current and accepted behavior in review.

- The repo's [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), [architecture](../architecture.md),
  [ADRs](../adr/) and [design system](../../frontend/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) record
  established decisions and conventions.
- The [development guide](../development.md) and CI workflow define the checks
  available today. The [OpenSpec workflow](../development.md#planning-changes)
  governs substantial new behavior or contract changes.
- Engineering standards and review prompts in this handbook apply to their
  stated scope. Significant changes to repository policy need approval.
  Record exceptions where the affected team can discover them and preserve
  the history of superseded decisions.
- The kit draws on external references such as OWASP for security and WCAG for
  accessibility. Use these as references for applicable work. A repository
  review covers only the behavior and checks actually examined.
